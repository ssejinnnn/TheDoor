The Door began as a collection of entrances photographed around New York City. Looking at them together, I became interested in how much a door can say about a building before we even enter it and experience the space inside.

Different materials, shapes, levels of transparency, and details can make an entrance feel open, private, formal, familiar, or controlled. The project looks at these differences through doors found across several neighborhoods and different parts of the city.

The website organizes the collection in different ways. The map shows where each entrance was found, while the archive brings the doors together as one larger visual collection of New York entrances.

The Make page uses the same collection in a more playful way, allowing different doors, details, and handles to be combined into a new entrance.
